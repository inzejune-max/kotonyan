About me

My name is Juliana, I am 37. I have lived in Tbilisi, in Bagebi, for four years with my husband. I work as a product manager for IT conferences, almost always from home, so I have the time and attention your cat deserves.

Cats have always been part of my life. My two cats lived with me into old age, so I know what daily care means, in sickness and in health. I do not have my own cat right now: we may relocate next year, and I would not be able to take an animal along at first. But I miss having cats around, so I am happy to help other people care for theirs.

I have a good feel for cat psychology. I never force contact, never startle, and give them time to get used to me. If the cat is ready, we play and I scratch behind the ears. If they want to be alone, I simply stay nearby and quietly do everything that needs doing.

About my experience. My experience is still modest, and I am honest about that. I have looked after my friends’ cats: at their homes, including overnight stays, and at mine. I know each of them by name and still keep up with how they are doing. You will meet them below.

Services and prices

Three formats, so you can pick what is calmer for the cat and easier for you.

At your home

50 GEL / day

I come to your cat and, if needed, stay overnight. The cat keeps its familiar territory, and you get peace of mind about the flat: I will leave it in perfect order when I go.

At my home

50 GEL / day

Boarding in Bagebi. A quiet flat with no other animals or children, prepared for a guest.

Day visits

40 GEL / day

One visit a day: feed, clean the litter box, change the water, play and send you photos.

Included in every format

  • Feeding on your schedule, with your food
  • Regular water changes
  • Daily litter box cleaning
  • Medication if needed: I have experience and do it as gently as possible, with minimal stress
  • Brushing, if you would like
  • Play and cuddles, if the cat does not mind
  • A photo update every day

Extras

A small discount is possible for 10+ days.

I can take your cat to the vet or groomer. This is paid separately, with a deposit for the specialist’s services and transport.

Food strictly as agreed with you. No “treats” of my own.

If your cat stays with me

It is just my husband and me, no children and no pets of our own. Your cat gets a calm flat where everything safe is allowed: sleeping on any surface, exploring the shelves, watching the street from the window.

How I prepare the flat for a guest

  • I clear all fragile, heavy and potentially dangerous items from open surfaces
  • Windows and balcony have mosquito screens, but while a cat is in the house, windows open only on the tilt latch
  • I feed only what and how we agreed with you
  • I never force contact: the cat decides when to come closer
